CivicLoop · Open-Spec Cooling & Town Water Tracker

Keep Town Water in Town While AI Racks Stay Cool.

“Pass the bill to shareholders and pass the savings onto the town.”

Some data center campuses consume hundreds of millions of gallons of local water a year, mostly through evaporative cooling. Closed-loop liquid cooling can use far less. CivicLoop lists the off-the-shelf parts inside closed-loop systems with distributor prices, tracks public-record water use town by town, and publishes a draft open reference design for engineers to review.

Methodology & engineering disclaimer: CivicLoop is independent and not affiliated with any company listed. Water figures come from the linked public sources; where a row multiplies a daily figure by 365 it says so. Calculator water estimates apply published WUE benchmarks (L per IT kWh) to a constant IT load and are order-of-magnitude only; real WUE depends on climate, set points, and design. Calculator dollar figures are rough illustrative estimates built from a few distributor list prices, are not quotes, and exclude major cost items listed with them. The reference skid is an unreviewed draft. Any real design should be reviewed by a qualified, licensed engineer in your jurisdiction. Prices and figures checked Oct 2026 and may change; corrections welcome.
